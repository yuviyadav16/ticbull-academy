Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

  • To personalize your learning experience and improve our AI doubt-solving capabilities.

  • To communicate with you regarding platform updates and support.

Data Security

We implement standard security measures to ensure the safety of your personal information. We do not sell or trade your personal identifiable information to outside parties.
